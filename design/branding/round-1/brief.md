# Keep — logo exploration, round 1

Keep is a personal API-key vault for developers. Its identity should feel calm,
precise, trustworthy, and approachable. The logo must read in the app header,
browser tab, and both themes. Name and current olive direction are retained as
assumptions; these are concepts for selection, not an approved replacement.

The current raster mark is a rounded K and dot on a black tile. This round
retains the short name while exploring a stronger standalone silhouette and
font-independent letterforms. No live application assets are replaced.

Category conventions to avoid: padlocks, shields, literal keys, blue security
badges, gradients, and generic initial letters inside rounded tiles.

Word map: keep → hold, tuck, retain, continuity; developer → precision, joint,
repeat; calm → generous counters, flat color, simple silhouette.

## Concept longlist

Scores: clarity / distinction / simplicity / relevance / small-size strength,
each out of five. Scores are design judgments, not research results.

Selected: split K (Latch), folded enclosure (Tuck), linked wordmark (Continuity).
All letters are drawn as paths; these concepts require no font to render.
Reference library studied for construction and distinction only, including
Kotlin, Dashlane, Adyen, and Patreon. This is not trademark clearance.
