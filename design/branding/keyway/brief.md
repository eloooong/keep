# Keep — website-led logo

Keep is a personal API-key vault for developers: encrypted storage, categories
and tags, controlled view/edit sharing, expiry reminders, and an activity trail.
The website is an operational tool with warm off-white surfaces, olive accents,
rounded controls, clear lowercase typography, and a quiet visual hierarchy.

The user's direction is to create a logo from that product and match the
existing website. This continues the two earlier concept rounds with a more
specific brief; it is not a new unrelated identity exercise.

**Core idea:** a key-shaped channel cut into a protective enclosure. The
negative space is the useful key; the solid perimeter is the place keeping it.
The open side suggests access through a deliberate path. Unequal teeth make
the silhouette distinctive without depicting every feature of the product.

**Constraints:** retain the name Keep and the incumbent palette. Calm, orderly,
precise, approachable. One-colour silhouette; vector geometry and no live text;
work at 16/24/32 px and in the actual desktop/mobile header in both themes.

## Shortlist behind the selected direction

Ideas considered across this round and earlier rounds: split K, folded
container, connected-e wordmark, paired brackets, nested key rings, enclosed
keyway, key teeth as shelves, a key with a squared bow, interlocking access
bands, and a compartment with a slotted opening. Enclosed keyway most directly
combines storage with credentials while remaining compact and quiet.

Category library checks included key/keyhole and developer-tools searches.
Avoid the circular keyhole of open-source marks, the key in a red diamond of
Passbolt, generic shield locks, and the keypad triangle of Vault. Library
references are construction comparisons only, never traced assets. This is
not trademark clearance.

The lowercase lettering is constructed from paths, using the custom Keep
letterform study from round 1, with optical spacing. No third-party font is
needed to display the logo. Application body typography remains Geist.
