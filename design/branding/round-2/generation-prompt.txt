Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: one polished square brand-identity exploration board containing FOUR radically different logo directions for the SAME brand, "keep", an API-key vault for developers. The client rejected safe geometric K logos and explicitly said "do it in a different way, go all out." Deliver ambitious, memorable identity design with impeccable professional logo craft. This is an exploratory raster board for selecting a direction, not a finished vector master.

Composition: spacious, beautifully art-directed 2 by 2 grid, four equal panels, edge-to-edge subtle colored backgrounds, a fine dividing rule. Each panel contains a large distinct symbol above or beside the lowercase word "keep", and a tiny quiet route label at its upper left. Clear hierarchy and generous negative space. All four marks should be visible and legible together. Within each panel place a small, understated black-and-white version of its symbol near the bottom to show it works without color. No physical merchandise mockups.

Explore these four visual territories with real sophistication:
01 / SENTINEL — a watchful small blackbird or raven in profile, simplified into a compact, sculptural, single-ink silhouette. Its wing and curled body protect a small diamond of negative space. Clever, bold, slightly mysterious, almost an archival printer's mark. Elegant typography with personality. Inky forest-black on warm pale parchment, with a restrained vermilion accent used only in layout. Bird must have anatomically coherent head, one beak, strong silhouette, no tiny feathers. It should be an original brand emblem, not clip art.
02 / CLASP — two broad organic bands clasping each other around a small off-center rectangular void, a sculptural abstract mark evoking holding something precious. It is NOT an infinity sign, chain link, padlock, or letter K. Precise yet warm. Bold dark aubergine on soft dusty lilac with a lime accent. Confident rounded custom grotesk wordmark.
03 / KEEPSAKE — typography-led identity: the word "keep" is the hero, with bespoke substantial lowercase letterforms, a surprising elegant shared counter or ligature between the two e's, and a memorable extended p detail. Refined, unusual, highly legible; create a real designed logotype, not ordinary font text. Rich burnt red on warm cream. Its small companion emblem is extracted from the distinctive double-e detail. Think enduring cultural identity, not default tech startup.
04 / COCOON — one compact smooth folded leaf or seed envelope as an abstract symbol, with a bright slit of negative space safely held inside. A beautiful, completely ownable asymmetric silhouette made from very few forms. It suggests safekeeping and a private interior without drawing literal security equipment. Acid yellow-green on near-black olive. A clean warm lowercase wordmark with excellent custom spacing.

Text must be exactly: the lowercase brand name "keep" in every panel; route labels "01 / SENTINEL", "02 / CLASP", "03 / KEEPSAKE", "04 / COCOON". No additional paragraphs, slogans, fake claims, watermarks, or extra words.

Quality constraints: flat, vector-like graphic design, clean filled silhouettes and crisp paths, optical balance, excellent kerning, deliberate figure-ground relationships. No stock K monograms, generic shields, keys, locks, cubes, folder icons, hexagons, gradients, shadows, glows, 3D, texture, pencil sketches, ornate decoration, or clutter. Each route must have its own strong personality. Prioritize an arresting, intelligent logo idea that remains clear when small. Whole board should feel like a celebrated independent identity studio presenting four exceptional fresh directions.
